About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, advised by Professor Ruixun Zhang.

Before that, I studied mathematics at the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China, with a concentration in probability and statistics.

My research interests include generative models, AI for finance, game theory, insurance and risk sharing, and decision-making under partial information.

Research Interests

  • Generative Models and Vision-Language Models
  • Artificial Intelligence for Finance
  • Game Theory and Reinforcement Learning
  • Insurance, Risk Sharing, and Partial Information
