Mathematics for intelligent decisions.

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, advised by Professor Ruixun Zhang.

My research connects modern generative AI with mathematical models for finance and strategic decision-making. I am especially interested in vision-language models, insurance and risk sharing, and games under partial information.
