About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, advised by Professor Ruixun Zhang.

Before that, I graduated from the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China, where I majored in Math in the Probability and Statistics track.

My research interests include generative models, AI for finance, and game theory and insurance.

Research Interests

  • Generative Models: Focus on large language models and diffusion models
  • AI for Finance
  • Game Theory and Insurance
